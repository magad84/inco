# INCO Terms of Use — V1 Launch Draft

**Status:** DRAFT FOR OWNER / LEGAL REVIEW  
**Date:** 2026-08-07  
**Applies to:** `https://mostafagad.net/inco`

> This draft defines the intended product boundary for INCO V1. It is not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal review. Governing law, jurisdiction, dispute wording, and final limitation-of-liability language remain owner/legal decisions.

## 1. Service

INCO is a free, limited professional decision-support service for international trade and logistics. It helps users identify missing information, operational risks, required confirmations, and practical next actions before relying on a shipment plan.

INCO does not provide a final carrier acceptance, customs approval, regulatory approval, permit, licence, dangerous-goods classification, HS classification, tax determination, legal opinion, insurance advice, live booking, live rate, live capacity, live schedule, cut-off confirmation, or clearance guarantee.

## 2. Preliminary Decision Support

INCO results are preliminary and depend on the facts entered, the scope of the available rules, and the freshness and applicability of the supporting sources.

Requirements may differ by product, route, carrier, customs procedure, jurisdiction, authority, shipment date, and other facts. Users must obtain current confirmation from the relevant carrier, customs authority, regulator, broker, dangerous-goods specialist, or other competent provider where the result indicates confirmation is required.

## 3. User Responsibility

Users are responsible for:

- providing accurate and materially complete shipment information;
- verifying current requirements before booking, dispatch, declaration, clearance, or other reliance;
- obtaining specialist or official confirmation where required;
- ensuring that their trade and shipment activities are lawful;
- not relying on INCO as a replacement for qualified operational, customs, regulatory, legal, tax, insurance, sanctions, or dangerous-goods review.

## 4. Prohibited Use

INCO must not be used to facilitate:

- false declarations or cargo misdescription;
- concealment of product identity, composition, origin, destination, value, parties, or hazards;
- sanctions or export-control evasion;
- unsafe shipment practices;
- illegal trade;
- fraudulent documentation;
- circumvention of carrier, customs, authority, safety, or compliance requirements.

## 5. Confidential and Personal Information

Do not enter names, account numbers, invoice numbers, banking details, passwords, credentials, confidential contracts, or unnecessary personal or proprietary information into the V1 shipment check.

The V1 deterministic shipment assessment is designed to operate in the browser without intentionally transmitting or persistently storing shipment-case values. Technical web-hosting and security logs may still process ordinary request metadata as described in the Privacy Notice.

## 6. Live and Volatile Information

Rates, capacity, schedules, cut-offs, booking status, route availability, permits, authority requirements, and carrier acceptance can change. INCO does not infer or guarantee live availability. A current dated source or provider confirmation is required when live information matters.

## 7. External Services and MyGPT

INCO may link to external services, including the INCO Assistant on ChatGPT. External services operate under their own accounts, availability, privacy practices, and service terms.

No shipment-case values or result content should be automatically transferred to MyGPT through the INCO website. Users should not share confidential information when using external services.
