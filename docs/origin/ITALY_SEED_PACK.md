# Italy Origin Country Seed Pack

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Research seed; not production-ready

## Scope

Covers export-origin questions for shipments moving from Italy to UAE, Saudi Arabia, Egypt, and Oman.

## Evidence Domains

- EU and Italian export declaration requirements.
- Export-control, dual-use, sanctions, and restricted-goods screening.
- Preferential and non-preferential origin evidence.
- Product conformity and supporting commercial documents.
- Dangerous-goods transport requirements by mode.
- Postal and express restrictions.
- Port, airport, rail, and inland-terminal capability.

## Required Inputs

- Exporter identity and customs registration status.
- Product description and HS candidate.
- Origin evidence and preference claim.
- End user and end use where controlled-goods indicators exist.
- Cargo nature, packaging, dimensions, and weight.
- Origin gateway and carrier service.
- Destination and transit route.

## Initial Gateway Candidates

### Maritime

- Genoa.
- La Spezia.
- Trieste.
- Venice.
- Livorno.
- Gioia Tauro.

### Air Cargo

- Milan Malpensa.
- Rome Fiumicino.
- Bologna.
- Venice Marco Polo.

Gateway inclusion is a discovery seed only and does not establish cargo acceptance or service availability.

## Elevated Checks

- Dual-use or controlled-technology indicators.
- Sanctions and restricted-party screening.
- Cultural goods, food, pharmaceuticals, chemicals, waste, and regulated machinery.
- Transit-country controls.

## Priority Research Backlog

1. Official Italian/EU export declaration and exporter-registration sources.
2. Current restricted and dual-use export controls.
3. Origin and preference evidence.
4. Official postal restrictions.
5. Gateway operator and cargo capability sources.
6. Direct and transshipment carrier services to launch markets.

## Safety Boundary

INCO returns `enhanced_compliance_screening_required` where product, party, end-use, destination, or transit indicators make a standard export check insufficient.
