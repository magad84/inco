# Australia Origin Country Seed Pack

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Research seed; not production-ready

## Scope

Covers export-origin questions for shipments from Australia to UAE, Saudi Arabia, Egypt, and Oman.

## Evidence Domains

- Export declaration and exporter responsibilities.
- Controlled and prohibited exports.
- Agricultural, food, livestock, plant, timber, and biosecurity evidence.
- Origin and certification requirements.
- Dangerous-goods transport evidence.
- Postal and express restrictions.
- Port, airport, and inland freight capability.

## Required Inputs

- Exporter identity.
- Product description and HS candidate.
- Commodity category and regulatory indicators.
- Agricultural, animal, plant, food, timber, or biological content.
- Permit, health, treatment, inspection, or certificate evidence.
- Dangerous-goods and special-cargo indicators.
- Origin gateway, carrier, service, transit, and destination.

Gateway inclusion is a discovery seed only and requires independent capability and service verification.

## Elevated Checks

- Food and agricultural exports.
- Meat, dairy, live animals, plants, seeds, and timber.
- Waste and environmental controls.
- Chemicals, batteries, and dangerous goods.
- Product-specific health or treatment certificates.

## Priority Research Backlog

3. Agriculture and biosecurity export evidence.

## Safety Boundary

INCO must not infer export readiness from a commercial invoice alone. Regulated commodities may require current permits, inspections, treatments, certificates, carrier acceptance, and destination-country confirmation.
