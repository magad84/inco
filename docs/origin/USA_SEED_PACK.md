# United States Origin Country Seed Pack

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Research seed; not production-ready

## Scope

Covers export-origin screening for shipments from the United States to UAE, Saudi Arabia, Egypt, and Oman.

## Evidence Domains

- Export filing and exporter-of-record responsibilities.
- Export-control classification and licensing.
- Restricted-party, end-user, and end-use screening.
- Sanctions and embargo controls.
- Product-specific agency requirements.
- Dangerous-goods transport evidence.
- Postal, express, air, and ocean carrier restrictions.
- Gateway capability and route availability.

## Required Inputs

- Exporter and ultimate consignee identity.
- Product description, HS candidate, and export-control indicators.
- End user and end use.
- Country of origin and re-export history where relevant.
- Value, quantity, and filing indicators.
- Dangerous-goods and special-cargo evidence.
- Origin gateway, carrier, service, transit, and destination.

## Enhanced Compliance Triggers

- Controlled technology or dual-use indicators.
- Military, nuclear, aerospace, encryption, or surveillance applications.
- Restricted party or ownership concerns.
- Diversion or unusual transshipment risk.
- Sanctioned-country or high-risk end-use nexus.

## Priority Research Backlog

1. Official export filing and exporter guidance.
2. Export-control classification and licensing sources.
3. Restricted-party and sanctions screening authorities.
4. Official postal and express prohibited/restricted rules.
5. Gateway operator sources and cargo capabilities.
6. Carrier service coverage to launch markets.
7. Product-agency requirements by cargo category.

## Safety Boundary

INCO must never reduce U.S. export compliance to HS code or destination alone. A result may require legal or specialist review when classification, party, end use, or licensing is uncertain.
