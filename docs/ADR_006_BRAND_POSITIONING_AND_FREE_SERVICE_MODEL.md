# ADR-006: Brand Positioning and Free Service Model

**Status:** Approved  
**Date:** 2026-08-06  
**Owner:** Mostafa Gad

## Decision

INCO will support the approved Mostafa Gad V3 brand positioning:

> **Business & Operations Leader with Deep Supply Chain Expertise**

The project must present Mostafa Gad primarily as a management, operations, and supply-chain expert. Technology, artificial intelligence, automation, product design, and open-source engineering are supporting execution capabilities. They are not the primary professional identity.

INCO will be launched as a real, free, limited professional service rather than as a demo, trial, paid SaaS, or software-development showcase.

## Brand Hierarchy

The approved hierarchy is:

1. Business and management leadership.
2. Operations as the executive core.
3. Supply Chain as the flagship deep expertise.
4. Governance, performance, transformation, research, and professional learning as supporting authority.
5. Applied AI and business automation as management enablers.

INCO must reinforce this hierarchy.

## Owner Role

Mostafa Gad is presented as:

- Founder;
- Product Owner;
- Domain Architect;
- Business-Rules Owner;
- Knowledge and Source-Governance Lead.

He must not be positioned primarily as:

- Software Developer;
- AI Engineer;
- Full-Stack Developer;
- Technical Specialist.

The repository demonstrates his ability to translate management and supply-chain expertise into governed tools, workflows, decision logic, and operational controls.

## Service Model

INCO is a free, limited decision-support service for:

- supply-chain and logistics professionals;
- procurement, trade, customs, and operations practitioners;
- entrepreneurs;
- small importers;
- individual importers and first-time importers.

The service objective is to:

- provide useful initial guidance;
- attract qualified professional traffic;
- strengthen trust in Mostafa Gad's expertise;
- connect users with relevant articles, research, books, videos, and professional pages;
- support professional reputation, speaking, teaching, collaboration, and career opportunities.

## Launch Boundary

The initial public service will be:

- free;
- anonymous;
- without registration;
- without payments;
- without saved cases;
- without document uploads;
- without live booking or rates;
- without final customs, carrier, authority, legal, insurance, or dangerous-goods approval.

The deterministic engine and bilingual templates will produce the result.

The AI explanation adapter remains in the codebase but is disabled by default for launch. It may be activated later only when actual usage demonstrates a clear benefit.

## Public Messaging

Public copy should describe INCO as:

> **Free International Trade and Logistics Decision Support**

Arabic direction:

> **أداة مجانية لدعم قرارات التجارة الدولية والشحن**

Public copy must not describe the service as a temporary demo or suggest that its purpose is to prove software-development skills.

## GitHub Positioning

The public repository is an open-source proof of:

- domain expertise;
- operational and management thinking;
- business-rule design;
- source governance;
- risk and uncertainty controls;
- product ownership;
- AI-enabled and automated execution under management governance.

GitHub is a supporting credibility channel, not the core professional identity.

## Commercial Boundary

No payment, subscription, or paid-report workflow is included in the approved launch scope.

Future consulting, training, speaking, teaching, private implementation, or professional collaboration may arise from reputation and inbound interest, but INCO itself remains a free limited service unless the owner approves a later change.

## Success Measures

- qualified visits to INCO and MostafaGad.net;
- search visibility for relevant management, operations, supply-chain, logistics, and trade topics;
- use of the free tool;
- visits to related articles, research, books, videos, and biography pages;
- professional follows, enquiries, invitations, collaborations, and career opportunities;
- GitHub engagement as supporting evidence of structured execution capability.
