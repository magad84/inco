# INCO Bilingual Terminology Register

**Version:** 0.1  
**Date:** 2026-08-05  
**Status:** Controlled working terminology for Arabic and English content

## 1. Purpose

This register keeps the Arabic and English product experience professionally equivalent.

Translations must preserve operational meaning, not merely match words literally.

## 2. Editorial Rules

- Use Modern Standard Arabic with clear professional logistics language.
- Avoid unnecessary transliteration when a standard Arabic term exists.
- Keep recognized three-letter trade-term abbreviations unchanged.
- Do not translate delivery as physical arrival when the underlying rule means contractual delivery at an earlier point.
- Keep risk, cost, responsibility, and operational control as separate concepts.
- Use one approved term consistently across UI, reports, rules, and help content.
- Do not use language that turns a preliminary indicator into an official determination.

## 12. Terms Requiring Contextual Review

The following must not be translated automatically without reviewing the exact rule or carrier context:

- Delivery.
- freight.
- handling.
- terminal charges.
- free time.
- demurrage.
- detention.
- storage.
- declaration.
- consignee.
- shipper.
- payload.
- dangerous, hazardous, prohibited, and restricted.
